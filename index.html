    Projects

    An Application of Clustering: Food Insecurity and Health in Western North Carolina

    Clustering is a data analysis technique that divides a dataset into distinct groups, with each group containing similar data points. We apply clustering to CDC social vulnerability and health conditions data to identify and map geographic regions in Western North Carolina that are most suitable for outreach efforts by Bounty and Soul: a local food bank which provides access to fresh produce and wellness education for individuals in Buncombe, Henderson, and McDowell counties.

    • This research was supported by a SEEDS grant from the South Big Data Innovation Hub.
